Starting strategy

Train on three nonconsecutive days each week. Add reps with good technique, then add resistance when you can. Aim to eat protein regularly and work toward more consistent sleep. Take comparison photos privately in your phone's camera, not here.

Plan B is a journal, not medical guidance. Your current routine is a starting point; adjust it when you acquire more equipment or progress.
